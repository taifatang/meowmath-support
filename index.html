FAQ

What is MeowMath?

A collection of everyday calculators, grouped into Tools, Motoring, and Finance — things like unit conversion, journey cost, real-life fuel efficiency, loan repayments, salary breakdowns, savings interest, and inflation. Enable only the ones you use.

How do I show or hide calculators?

Go to the Settings tab and toggle any calculator on or off. Only enabled calculators appear in the Tools tab. You can turn all of them off — the Tools tab will show you how to turn them back on.

Where does my calculation history go?

The History tab keeps your recent calculations, searchable by calculator. You can set a cap on how many entries are kept, and copy a past result at any time.

Is my data private?

Everything stays on your device. No accounts, no tracking, no servers. Your numbers never leave your phone.

Why are results rounded?

Figures are rounded to two decimal places to stay readable. That's plenty precise for everyday use, though it may differ slightly from an exact, unrounded figure.
