Your calculations

The numbers you enter and the calculation history you keep are stored only on your device. MeowMath has no accounts and no servers of its own. That part of the app never sends your data anywhere.

Advertising

MeowMath shows ads through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). To serve and measure ads, the SDK can collect and send the following to Google:

DataPurpose
Device ID (advertising identifier)Ad personalisation & measurement
IP address / approximate (coarse) locationAd targeting & measurement
Advertising data (ads shown, clicks)Ad measurement
Product interaction (app launches, taps)Ad measurement
Crash & performance dataDiagnostics for the ad SDK

This is covered by Google's own privacy policy and ad policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data from partner sites and apps

Your controls

  • On iOS, you can allow or deny tracking via the App Tracking Transparency prompt, or later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • You can reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device's ad settings at any time.
